How almost every scam works

Different stories, same playbook. Once you see the pattern, you can spot it.

1

The Hook

A message, call or ad grabs your attention: a prize, a job, a problem with your account.

2

The Pressure

Fear, excitement or urgency stops you from thinking clearly. "Act now!"

3

The Ask

Click a link, share an OTP, install an app, or transfer money.

4

The Disappearance

Once they have your money or details, they block you and vanish.
