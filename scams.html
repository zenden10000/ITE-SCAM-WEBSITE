Anyone can be a target

These stories are illustrative, but based on common real-world cases.

W
"I thought it was an easy part-time job. After the first $20 payout I trusted them and topped up $1,800 to 'unlock' tasks. Then they disappeared." Wei, 19, student (job scam)
T
"The caller knew my name and IC number. He said I was involved in money laundering. I was so scared I did everything he said." Mdm Tan, 67, retiree (government impersonation)
D
"I work in IT. I still clicked a parcel delivery link when I was tired after work. It only takes one moment." Daniel, 34, engineer (phishing)
P
"We chatted for three months. When he needed money for a 'customs fee', it felt like helping someone I loved." Priya, 45, teacher (romance scam)
